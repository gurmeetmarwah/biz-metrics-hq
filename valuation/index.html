What Buyers Weigh: Cash Flow, Margins & Growth

Understanding cash flow vs profitability for owners—and the other factors brokers use when pricing a business.

  • Recurring Revenue

    Maintenance plans and subscription contracts command premium multiples at exit.

  • Cash Flow vs Profitability

    Buyers price durable cash—not just accounting profit. Understanding cash flow vs profitability for owners helps you present a cleaner, more bankable story at sale.

  • Customer Retention

    Low churn signals durable cash flow and reduces buyer risk.

  • Growth Rate

    Consistent year-over-year revenue growth supports higher valuation ranges.

  • Profit Margin

    Strong SDE margins relative to industry peers increase buyer confidence.

  • Owner Dependence

    Transferable management and systems reduce key-person discount.
