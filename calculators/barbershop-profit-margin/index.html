Calculation formula

  • Net profit margin

    Net Margin = (Revenue − Payroll − Product COGS − Rent − Marketing − Overhead) ÷ Revenue × 100

    Healthy barbershop net margin: 12–20%; payroll typically 40–48% of revenue.

Limitations

  • Barbershop economics vary widely by location, walk-in vs appointment mix, and booth-rent vs commission model.
  • Franchise and modern grooming concepts carry different cost structures than traditional shops.
  • Figures are directional ranges — not substitutes for your own shop management data.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the average profit margin for a barbershop?

The average barbershop profit margin runs 12–20% net, with a median around 16%. Shops that keep payroll near 40–48% of revenue and drive high chair utilization can push net margins toward the top of the range.

What are the biggest costs for a barbershop?

Barber payroll (40–48%), product and consumables (4–7%), marketing (4–6%), rent (10–15%), and retail inventory are the largest barbershop expenses. Controlling payroll and maximizing chair utilization are the highest-impact margin levers.
